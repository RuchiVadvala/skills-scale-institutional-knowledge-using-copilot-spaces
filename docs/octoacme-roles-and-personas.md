# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They work closely with developers and product managers to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for each release
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria checklists
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate features against acceptance criteria before handoff to product
- Identify and log defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in release readiness reviews and smoke testing

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable releases to production
- Reduce post-release defects and incidents
- Enable fast, confident deployments through clear quality signals

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (quality input)
- Daily standups (quality blockers and test status)
- Release readiness sign-off meetings

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Collaborate on test strategy, receive bug reports and reproduction steps, validate fixes
- **Product Managers:** Align on acceptance criteria, validate feature completeness before release
- **Project Managers:** Report quality status and blockers in daily standups and weekly syncs

---

## Business Owner / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Business Owners define strategic priorities, secure resources, and serve as the voice of the business. They provide executive sponsorship and final approval authority for major initiatives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Prioritize initiatives and allocate budget/resources
- Serve as executive sponsor and escalation point
- Approve major scope changes and trade-offs
- Communicate business impact to broader organization

### Goals
- Align technical delivery with business strategy
- Maximize return on investment
- Reduce business risk and ensure customer satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and business reviews
- Milestone sign-offs and gate reviews
- Escalation for high-impact decisions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Provide strategic direction, approve scope changes and trade-off decisions, receive escalations
- **Product Managers:** Define business objectives and success metrics, collaborate on prioritization and ROI
- **All Team Members:** Receive quarterly/milestone business impact updates and celebrate wins

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system and component architecture, evaluate technology choices, and ensure scalability, performance, and maintainability. They provide technical leadership and strategic guidance throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and propose architectural improvements
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Advise on technology and platform decisions
- Ensure consistency with architectural standards and best practices
- Support capacity planning and technical roadmap
- Validate technical feasibility and scalability of proposed solutions

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant systems
- Reduce long-term technical debt
- Enable rapid iteration without compromising quality

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Planning phase (technical feasibility and risk assessment)
- Ad-hoc technical advisory during execution
- Technical risk identification and mitigation planning

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Provide architectural guidance and design reviews, ensure adherence to standards
- **Project Managers:** Advise on technical risks and feasibility, participate in planning and dependency management
- **Product Managers:** Evaluate technical feasibility of feature requests, inform product roadmap with technical capabilities
